Riverside Boat Safari

Embark on an enchanting journey through Sri Lanka’s tranquil rivers and vibrant mangroves. Glide along the serene waters of the Bentota or Madu River, where lush greenery and exotic wildlife await. Choose a morning safari to witness herons, kingfishers, and crocodiles in their natural habitat, or a sunset cruise to capture the golden hues of dusk over Cinnamon Island. Guided by our expert local navigators, immerse yourself in the rich biodiversity and cultural heritage of these iconic waterways for a truly unforgettable adventure.
